# Technical / system-design questions

Technical-lite questions for companies that include a technical round (Uber,
Google PM, Stripe, Coinbase). Not product-design or behavioral; candidates
are expected to reason about systems and trade-offs, not design distributed
systems. Content-seeded from real interviews (see `data/benchmarks/`). Wired
into the interview flow (dimension mode, 1 question per interview) in
`server/config/stage_order.md`; rubric in `rubric.md`.

1. Explain how Netflix works.
    - Sourced: Google/Uber technical, benchmark 02
2. Explain how UberPool's pooling algorithm works to a five-year-old.
    - Sourced: benchmark 02
3. Open the Uber app and talk me through the information architecture.
    - Sourced: benchmark 02
4. How familiar are you with open-source technologies?
    - Sourced: benchmark 02
5. How does rider-driver matching work at scale, and what are the trade-offs between speed, accuracy, and cost?
    - Sourced: Uber, benchmark 05
6. How do you deliver real-time location and trip updates across thousands of cities without hurting reliability?
    - Sourced: Uber, benchmark 05
7. Gift card redemption must stay fast and fraud-resistant. What are the product and system implications?
    - Sourced: Uber, benchmark 05
