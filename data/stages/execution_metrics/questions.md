Don't fit the existing stages cleanly: they are metric-centric, not full
design or decision cases. Content-seeded from real interviews (see
`data/benchmarks/`). Wired into the interview flow (dimension mode, 2
questions minimum per interview, no ceiling) in `server/config/stage_order.md`; rubric in
`rubric.md`.
