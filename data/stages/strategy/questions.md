# Strategy questions

Forward-looking, competitive, and business-model questions that don't fit the
case_study "diagnose and respond" type. Content-seeded from real interviews
(see `data/benchmarks/`). Wired into the interview flow (dimension mode, 2
questions minimum per interview, no ceiling) in `server/config/stage_order.md`; rubric in
`rubric.md`.

1. What's the biggest threat to YouTube?
    - Sourced: Google, benchmark 02
2. How would you react to a product competing with Gmail?
    - Sourced: Google, benchmark 02
3. If you were the CEO of Apple, what product would you eliminate from the lineup?
    - Sourced: Apple, benchmark 07
4. If you were the CEO of LEGO, what new product line would you create to increase revenue?
    - Sourced: Apple/Capital One, benchmark 07
5. How would you bootstrap a product that helps people find apartments?
    - Sourced: Capital One, benchmark 07
