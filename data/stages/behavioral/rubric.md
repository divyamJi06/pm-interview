## Intent

Probe whether the candidate can lead and create impact in messy,
people-heavy situations without relying on formal authority; validate the
depth behind resume claims.

## Ideal structure

1. Context
2. Challenge/tension
3. Goal
4. Actions + reasoning
5. Influence
6. Result
7. Reflection

## Scoring dimensions

- Ownership & agency — personally drove something vs. describing what the team did
- Influence & leadership — moves people without positional authority, especially under disagreement
- Judgment & reasoning — actions deliberate, grounded in data/context/people's motivations
- Impact & scale — actions materially changed the outcome, at appropriate significance
- Learning & adaptability — honest reflection: what changed, what they'd do differently

## Green flags

- first-person ownership language ("I decided", "I persuaded")
- consequential example, not trivial
- explicit conflict/constraint stated
- explains why, not just chronology
- shows influence tactics (data, incentives, trust, allies)
- acknowledges others' perspectives
- quantifies/concretizes outcome
- handles follow-ups without the story breaking
- specific, updated reflection

## Red flags

- "we"-heavy, unclear personal contribution
- describes the situation, not the decision-making
- equates leadership with authority ("I told them")
- claims influence without explaining how
- data used as decoration
- blames others instead of showing empathy
- context-heavy but low tension/impact
- no trade-offs, always right
- vague result disconnected from actions
- can't explain reasoning or hindsight
- superficial or manufactured reflection
- example too small in scale
