# Statistical & probabilistic reasoning

- Thinking in Bets — Annie Duke — judgment under uncertainty, separating luck from skill and committing to decisions in ambiguous environments
- The Signal and the Noise — Nate Silver — calibration, base rates, and why forecasts fail and how to sharpen them
- How to Measure Anything — Douglas W. Hubbard — applied decision theory: estimate ranges, break unknowns down, quantify risks in interviews
- Khan Academy / 3Blue1Brown: Probability & Statistics — free, fast refresher on distributions, sampling, and conditional probability for estimation

# MLOps & production ML for non-ML PMs

- Designing Machine Learning Systems — Chip Huyen — the full lifecycle: data quality, training-serving skew, drift, and evaluation design
- www.ml-engineering.guide — Andrej Karpathy — practical deep-learning infrastructure for engineers and PMs
- Feature Store / model monitoring playbooks — retraining cadence, drift thresholds, and who owns the SLA between the model and the product team

# Business modeling & GTM

- The Lean Startup — Eric Ries — build-measure-learn loops and validated learning around new ventures
- Crossing the Chasm — Geoffrey A. Moore — segmentation and go-to-market sequencing for technology products
- TAM/SAM/SOM and unit-economics calculators (Sequoia / A16Z templates) — bottom-up vs top-down sizing, CAC/LTV, payback period, gross margin
- Good to Great / Blue Ocean Strategy — positioning and differentiation when markets are crowded

# Product discovery & UX research

- The Mom test — Rob Fitzpatrick — running honest conversations with customers, avoiding leading questions
- Sprint — Jake Knapp (GV) — one-week process for mapping, prototyping, and testing product ideas
- Measuring and optimizing funnel + session analytics (Amplitude / Mixpanel playbooks) — activation, retention, and the loop from data back to discovery

# Feasibility, risk & decision-making under uncertainty

- Decisive — Chip & Dan Heath — broadening options, testing assumptions, and making trade-off calls under time pressure
- How to Solve It — George Pólya — systematic problem decomposition for ambiguous multi-part cases
- Risk management frameworks (impact x likelihood matrices, premortems) — structuring the "what could go wrong and what do we do about it" answer in case studies

# Stakeholder influence & communication

- Crucial Conversations — Patterson et al. — high-stakes conversations when opinions differ and risks are high
- Radical Candor — Kim Scott — direct feedback without being a jerk, useful for pushback with engineering and executives
- Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion — Robert Cialdini — reciprocity, commitment, and social proof for getting alignment without authority
- Amazon's Six-Page Memos / Great Communicators playbook — writing to a decision, crisp narrative structure

# Behavioral storytelling (STAR) for PM interviews

- The STAR Method practice deck (Amazon/Google interview guides) — Situation, Task, Action, Result with quantified outcomes
- Switch — Chip & Dan Heath — structuring stories around a clear change arc that interviewers can follow
- Resumes & behavioral interview frameworks that force "What did YOU do, not the team" — personal accountability framing

# Quantitative reasoning / estimation drills

- CaseInterview.com and Cosmiq estimation drills — daily practice on market sizing and Fermi problems
- Facebook/Celonis estimation frameworks — top-down vs bottom-up, sanity-checks with known comparators, stating error bars
