# 08 — Cross-company estimation & growth case bank

- Fetched: 2026-09 (hackingthecaseinterview.com PM case-study guide — page
  itself returns 403 on fetch; excerpts taken from search results)
  - **Role:** PM case studies (product sense), widely asked at Google, Uber,
    TikTok, Meta, Amazon.

## Real estimation questions reported

- How many requests per second does Gmail handle?
- What is the TAM for e-scooters in New York City?
- How much daily revenue does YouTube generate?
- How many piano tuners in the United States? (classic)
- Market-sizing variants ("How many cups of coffee sold in India per day" etc.)

## Real growth/product-metrics questions reported

- How would you increase Instagram's daily active users (DAU)?
- How would you grow LinkedIn's revenue?
- How would you increase engagement on TikTok?
- Pair each with: pick a north-star metric and 2-3 supporting metrics; identify
  the lever that moves each supporting metric.

## The framework real candidates are expected to show

- For estimation: clarify scope → break into population/subsets → reasonable
  base rates → sanity-check with a different approach → state a number.
- For product/growth: north-star → decompose into 2-3 supporting metrics →
  map behavioral levers → hypotheses/experiments → guardrail metrics.

## What this says about our mock

- Our estimation stage (checklist mode, 8-step) already matches the real
  expectation. The reported question set (Gmail QPS, NYC e-scooter TAM, YouTube
  revenue) overlaps the style of our 11 questions; we can add a couple of
  Gmail/YouTube/indie-style ones to cover the US/market-sizing side.
- The growth side (north-star + 2-3 supporting metrics + experiments) has no
  dedicated stage in our mock — nearest is case_study. A "execution/metrics"
  optional stage would mirror Google/Meta/Uber; strong candidate for a company
  preset feature.
- Metric choice discipline (2-3 supporting, not a list of 8) is a rubric
  improvement: our case_study Judge could penalize metric-dumps.
