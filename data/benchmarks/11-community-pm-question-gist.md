## Content summary

- A long, categorized list of real PM questions collected from candidate
  reports: behavioral ("tell me about a time..."), product design, product
  strategy, estimation / market sizing, metrics, and prioritization.
- Notably for our project, the estimation category contains exactly the
  questions we added to our own bank:
  - How many police officers are there in the US?
  - How many basketballs are purchased every year in the US?
  - How much does the US spend on dog food each year?
  - How many tennis balls can fit in a two-bedroom apartment?
  - (plus shampoo-market-scale style questions)
- This is independent validation that our new estimation questions (lines
  7-11 of `data/stages/estimation/questions.md`) match questions real
  candidates are actually asked.

## What this says about our mock

- Our estimation bank is now aligned with reality for the US-popular subset.
- The list also reinforces that behavioral breadth is the biggest content gap
  (see `09-behavioral-question-bank.md`).
