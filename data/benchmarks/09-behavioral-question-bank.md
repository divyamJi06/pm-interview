# 09 — Cross-company behavioral question bank (495+)

- Fetched: 2026-09 (Exponent/Aced behavioral bank; ProductSchool; Mississippi
  State career; prodpad)
  - **Role:** general PM behavioral, all companies.

## The highest-frequency real questions

**Openers / self:**
- Tell me about yourself.
- What is your favorite product and why?
- Why do you want to switch jobs now?

**Past behavior (STAR / SPSIL targets):**
- Tell me about a time you handled a difficult stakeholder.
- Tell me about a time one of your products failed.
- Tell me about a time you made short-term sacrifices for long-term gains.
- Tell me about a product decision that made you genuinely sad or frustrated.
- Tell me about a time you tried to convince your manager and were unsuccessful.
- Tell me about a time you had a conflict; how did you resolve it and what did
  you learn?
- Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
- Tell me about a time you took a calculated risk when speed was critical.
- Tell me about a time your project failed.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone and how you resolved it.
- Tell me about a time you solved a complex problem.

**Leadership / cross-functional:**
- Tell me about a time you raised the bar.
- How do you respond if your team disagrees with your ideas?
- Tell me about a time you had to balance multiple stakeholders with
  conflicting needs.
- How do you say no to feature requests?
- Tell me about a time you stepped up into a leadership role.
- How have you built trust during significant strategy shifts?

**Product-work evidence:**
- Tell me about a product you launched from idea to market.
- Tell me about a time you changed the direction of the organization.
- How do you demonstrate success without concrete results to share?
- Have you directly spoken with customers yourself?

**Culture / fit:**
- Why do you think we should not hire you? (reported often)
- Legal has concerns about your feature. What do you do?
- What do you find most challenging about being a PM?
- How have you adopted AI in your workflows? (new, 2025-26)

## Patterns: what real behavioral interviews share

- Every "tell me about a time" is followed by *specific* digging: role vs team,
  numbers, what changed, what you'd do differently.
- Companies bias their emphasis: Amazon = LPs; Meta = influence without
  authority + saying no; Netflix = ownership + candor; Google = ambiguity.
- Candidates who answer "we" instead of "I" get flagged as not owning outcomes.

## What this says about our mock

- Our behavioral bank is small (a handful of questions). Real interviews draw
  from a 500+ space; we can grow the bank cheaply and tag each question with
  company flavor (Amazon LP, Meta influence, Netflix candor).
- The "we vs I" ownership check is a concrete new intent dimension for the
  behavioral Judge.
- "Say no with rationale", "failed product", "difficult stakeholder", and
  "calculated risk" are the highest-frequency gaps in our current bank.
